## Step-by-step troubleshooting for how to reset hp printer to factory settings

### 1. Check the power source

Connect the printer directly to a known-working wall outlet where practical. Inspect the cable and adapter for visible damage; do not use damaged equipment or open the power supply.

### 2. Perform a normal restart

Turn the printer off using its power button, wait for it to shut down fully, and disconnect power only when the manual permits it. Reconnect and start it once the recommended wait has passed.

### 3. Separate reset types

A power cycle, network reset, settings reset, and factory reset do different things. A factory reset may erase Wi-Fi details, custom settings, address books, or administrator configuration.

### 4. Follow the exact model guide

Use the official manual for the model's button sequence or menu path. Avoid combinations found on random forums, since some can start service or maintenance modes.

### 5. Know when to stop

If there is a burning smell, liquid damage, repeated power loss, unusual heat, or no response from a known-good outlet, disconnect it if safe and arrange qualified service.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not force a cartridge, drum, paper tray, or cover into place. Avoid scraping printheads, touching a drum's imaging surface, pouring cleaning fluid into the machine, or dismantling hot or high-voltage components. Do not download firmware or drivers from unofficial sites, and never interrupt a firmware update simply because the display appears slow. Repeated cleaning cycles can consume ink, while an unnecessary factory reset can remove network and custom settings. Check the model's official manual before any procedure that changes settings or opens the device.
