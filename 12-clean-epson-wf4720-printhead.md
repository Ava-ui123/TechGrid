## Step-by-step troubleshooting for cleaning the printhead on epson wf4720

### 1. Check supplies and settings

Confirm the ink or toner is compatible and installed correctly. Review the printer's displayed supply status, paper type, quality mode, and whether grayscale or draft printing is selected.

### 2. Print a built-in test

Use the printer's own nozzle check, quality report, or configuration page. This helps distinguish a printer-side issue from a document, application, or computer driver problem.

### 3. Run only the recommended maintenance

Use the built-in cleaning, alignment, or calibration utility for your exact model. Follow on-screen prompts and allow the process to finish before starting another cycle.

### 4. Inspect replaceable parts carefully

Follow the model manual when checking cartridges, toner, or a drum unit. Avoid touching imaging surfaces, printhead nozzles, electrical contacts, or hot components unless the manual explicitly directs you.

### 5. Test with known-good paper

Load clean, supported paper and print a simple page. If the fault appears only in one application or file, check its color settings, page layout, and driver before replacing hardware.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not force a cartridge, drum, paper tray, or cover into place. Avoid scraping printheads, touching a drum's imaging surface, pouring cleaning fluid into the machine, or dismantling hot or high-voltage components. Do not download firmware or drivers from unofficial sites, and never interrupt a firmware update simply because the display appears slow. Repeated cleaning cycles can consume ink, while an unnecessary factory reset can remove network and custom settings. Check the model's official manual before any procedure that changes settings or opens the device.
